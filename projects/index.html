SNP Bind-n-Seq Quantifying TF-DNA binding affinities at non-coding variants

An in vitro SELEX-based platform (Bind-n-Seq) for measuring how single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) alter the DNA binding affinity of transcription factors. Used to systematically evaluate non-coding variants associated with congenital heart disease and interpret GWAS signals at cardiac TF binding sites.

GKM-SVM for TF Binding Gapped k-mer support vector machine for predicting TF binding sites

Application and extension of the GKM-SVM machine learning framework to predict transcription factor binding sites from sequence alone. Enables genome-wide scoring of regulatory variants and integration with GWAS data to prioritize functionally relevant non-coding SNPs in cardiovascular disease loci.

machine-learning sequence-motifs regulatory-genomics gkm-svm
CREPE Comprehensive Regulatory Element Prediction Engine

A computational pipeline integrating high-throughput sequencing data, TF binding models, and functional genomics annotations to predict and prioritize regulatory elements. Designed for use with MPRA (Massively Parallel Reporter Assay) data to link sequence variants to transcriptional activity in cardiac cell types.

regulatory-elements mpra bioinformatics-pipeline functional-genomics
NKX2-5 / TBX5 Biophysics Structural and thermodynamic dissection of cardiac TF-DNA interactions

Combining EMSA, fluorescence anisotropy, and footprinting assays with molecular modeling to characterize how NKX2-5, TBX5, and GATA4 bind their DNA targets individually and cooperatively. Structural insights inform how disease-associated mutations alter DNA recognition and regulatory network connectivity in the developing heart.
