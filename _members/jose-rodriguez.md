José A. Rodríguez-Martínez is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biology at the University of Puerto Rico Río Piedras. His research focuses on the molecular mechanisms by which transcription factors (TFs) recognize DNA and how non-coding genetic variants alter TF-DNA interactions to influence gene regulation in human disease. His lab integrates biochemical approaches (SELEX, EMSA, MPRA) with computational genomics (GKM-SVM, machine learning) to decode the cis-regulatory grammar of the cardiac transcriptional network. A central goal is to understand how variants in regulatory elements contribute to congenital heart disease and cardiovascular traits identified by GWAS.
