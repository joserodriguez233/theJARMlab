{% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

The JARMlab investigates how transcription factors (TFs) interpret the genetic information encoded in DNA regulatory elements, and how non-coding genetic variants disrupt these interactions to contribute to human disease. Using an integrative combination of biophysical experiments — including Bind-n-Seq, EMSA, fluorescence anisotropy, and MPRA — and computational genomics approaches, we decode the regulatory logic of the non-coding genome, with a primary focus on transcription factors implicated in cardiovascular disease such as NKX2-5, TBX5, and GATA4.
